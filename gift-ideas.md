# Gift Ideas — 1st Birthday (Girl)

Edit this list freely — trim it down, reorder, add real links once you pick items.
Organized so you can pick a handful per category rather than register for everything.

## Everyday / Practical
- Clothing, next size up (12–18mo or 18–24mo) — everyday basics, one nice outfit
- First real shoes (soft-sole, for new walkers)
- Silicone suction plate/bowl + toddler utensil set
- Open-top sippy cup or straw cup (transition away from bottles)
- Bibs (silicone, easy-wipe)
- Noise-reducing headphones/earmuffs (great for a social baby at loud parties, concerts, fireworks)
- Humidifier for her room
- Sun hat + swimsuit for next summer

## Toys & Development
- Wooden stacking rings / shape sorter
- Push walker (helps new walkers balance)
- Ride-on toy (no pedals yet, just scoot)
- Mini musical instruments (drum, xylophone, shaker eggs) — good fit for a social, expressive baby
- Sensory bin materials (textured balls, fabric scraps, safe household objects)
- Bath toys (stacking cups, squirters)
- Bubble machine (great for outdoor parties, very "happy baby" energy)
- Small indoor slide or soft climbing set

## Books
- Sturdy board books (touch-and-feel, first words, animals)
- Personalized name book
- Fabric/cloth books for chewing-stage babies

## Keepsakes & Sentimental
- Handprint/footprint keepsake kit
- Growth chart (wall decal or wooden ruler style)
- Engraved jewelry/keepsake box
- Custom illustrated portrait of her
- "First year" memory box or shadow box
- Framed print from the birthday party

## Experiences (fits a social, people-loving baby)
- Membership: children's museum, zoo, or aquarium
- Mommy-and-me swim or music class package
- Professional photo session (family or milestone shoot)
- Gift card for a family outing (petting zoo, indoor play gym)

## Small-Business / Etsy-Style Finds
- Personalized blanket or robe with her name
- Custom wooden name puzzle
- Embroidered outfit or hat

## Bigger-Ticket (good for pooling money from multiple guests)
- Convertible car seat (next stage up)
- Toddler learning tower / kitchen helper stool
- Small outdoor playset or backyard sandbox

## Non-Toy Options
- Contribution to her 529 college savings fund
- Savings bond or custodial investment account
- Donation to a children's charity in her name

---
**Where to look beyond Amazon:** Etsy (personalized/handmade), Babylist (lets you add items from any store into one registry), Target/Pottery Barn Kids/Nordstrom registries, local baby boutiques, and secondhand/consignment shops for gently-used big-ticket items like play gyms or ride-on toys.
